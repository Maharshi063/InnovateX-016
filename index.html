The Underlying Problem

Your email address can become a permanent identifier.

People reuse the exact same personal email address across newsletters, shopping portals, events, downloads, and trials. Once shared, you lose visibility into how that identity is stored, brokered, or leaked.

Persistent Fingerprint

Cross-site trackers correlate profiles by using your constant personal email.

Zero Leak Attribution

When spam arrives, it's impossible to know which service leaked your address.

No Targeted Revocation

You cannot shut down spam without abandoning your primary inbox completely.
